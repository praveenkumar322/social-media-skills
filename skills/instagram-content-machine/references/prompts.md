# Instagram prompts (E82 edition)

Adapted by E82 Studios (@e82studios) from a public "8 Claude prompts for Instagram growth" thread (author unknown; seven prompts were shared). Changes from the originals: each prompt reads E82's own files first, follows the account's style rules, keeps numbers out of the model's hands, and keeps a person in charge of posting and DMs.

Load first, every time: the account's `about-me.md`, `voice.md`, offer facts and style rules. For E82 these are `operating/who-we-are.md`, `operating/quarter.md`, `operating/style-rules.md`, `shared/business-facts.json` and `operating/instagram/README.md` in the n8n-e82Agents repo.

## 1. The Instagram blueprint
```
Act as an Instagram growth strategist for E82 Studios (@e82studios). Read the files listed above first.
Build a practical Instagram plan that turns the account into a source of discovery calls.
1. Audience: who on Instagram buys what the business facts list. Pick one primary audience; say who we skip.
2. Content: 5 pillars, Reel ideas for each, and how we ask for saves, shares and comments.
3. Growth: a step-by-step organic system that fits 3 hours a week.
4. Clients: how a follower becomes a booked call (point at the audit and the booking page).
5. Workflow: which steps an agent drafts and which the founder does by hand. Nothing posts or sends on its own.
6. Standing out: what similar studios and agencies post, and the gap E82 can own.
7. 90 days: a weekly roadmap with tasks and the KPIs to read from Instagram Insights. Give targets as ranges labelled "estimate", never as promises.
No generic advice. Everything must be something we can start this week.
```

## 2. 100 content ideas
```
Act as an Instagram content strategist for E82 Studios. Read the files listed above first.
Give 100 post ideas across our 5 pillars: Reels, carousels and Stories. For each: format, a hook under 12 words, the one point it makes, and one CTA (save, comment a keyword, or link in bio).
Make every idea something one person can film or design in under 45 minutes. Mark the 10 you'd make first and say why.
```

## 3. 30-day calendar
```
Act as an Instagram growth planner for E82 Studios. Read the files listed above first.
Build a 30-day calendar on our cadence (3 Reels, 1 carousel, Stories on 5 days a week).
Each day: format, pillar, hook, a caption draft in our voice, and the CTA.
Put the strongest proof posts on the days we also do outreach, so prospects who check our profile find them.
```

## 4. 20 Reel scripts
```
Act as an Instagram Reels scriptwriter for E82 Studios. Read the files listed above first.
Write 20 Reel scripts, 20-45 seconds each. Each script: the hook (first 2 seconds, on-screen text + spoken line), the beats with a pattern interrupt every 5-7 seconds, the payoff, and one CTA.
Tell real process stories (a task before and after). No invented client results: where a number is needed, leave [CLIENT FIGURE] for me to fill from the client's own data.
```

## 5. Growth system
```
Act as an Instagram growth strategist for E82 Studios. Read the files listed above first.
Design our follower growth system: content, daily engagement (which accounts to comment on and how), collaborations (Collab posts with clients, partners and local business creators), and Stories that move people to the profile.
Real followers only: no follow/unfollow, no engagement pods, no bought followers, nothing that breaks Instagram's terms.
```

## 6. Followers into clients
```
Act as a sales funnel strategist for E82 Studios. Read the files listed above first.
Build the path from Instagram follower to paying client: the content that earns trust, the lead magnet (the automation audit tool on our site), the offer (the fixed-price audit from the business facts), comment-keyword triggers, and DM reply drafts for the founder to send by hand.
Write the DM drafts under 100 words, first sentence answering their question, per our style rules.
```

## 7. Competitor review
```
Act as an Instagram strategist for E82 Studios. Read the files listed above first.
I'll paste 5-10 accounts we compete with or admire, plus their recent top posts. From that material only:
what they post, how they hook, how they get engagement, how they sell. Then name the gaps we can own and a step-by-step plan to stand apart.
Don't guess follower counts or results you weren't given. Say "not in the material" instead.
```
